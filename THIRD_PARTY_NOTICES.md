# Third-party material and excluded files

The MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants apply only to rights held by this project's authors.
They do not replace an upstream licence or imply upstream endorsement.

- Sentinel-1-derived radar measurements: credit Copernicus Sentinel data and
  Google Earth Engine, as documented in the export scripts and protocol. The
  exports and processed results are not ownership claims over the source imagery.
- RiceAtlas, the Chen et al. India rice product, ESA WorldCereal 2021, and the
  terrain datasets: retain each source's attribution and conditions. The source
  publications, collection identifiers, selection rules and derivations are in
  `docs/Protocol_v1.md` and `gee/`. Source product archives are not included here.
- MARC: cite the source authors and dataset (DOI 10.17595/20230728.001; the
  associated paper is DOI 10.5194/essd-16-3893-2024). The MARC source archive is
  not included in this Git repository. Its original CC BY 4.0 attribution must
  accompany any permitted redistribution in the separate data deposit.
- IBTrACS: retain NOAA/NCEI and source-agency attribution and the recorded
  version/subset. This repository includes derived exposure tables, not the raw
  IBTrACS input archive. Consult the protocol for the source reference.
- GADM: source shapefiles are excluded. Academic-use map outlines do not confer
  permission to redistribute the source geometry. Obtain source data directly
  under https://gadm.org/license.html.
- Nine third-party reference PDFs formerly held in the private development
  repository are excluded from this clean repository and its history. Source
  citations and links remain in `docs/field_reference_sources.md`; statements
  there about local archival copies describe the private research archive.
  ICAR-CRIDA and PMFBY material must not be added without appropriate permission:
  https://www.icar-crida.res.in/disclaimer.html and
  https://pmfby.gov.in/copyrightPolicy.

Quotations and citations in the historical research record are attributed to
their original sources and are not offered under a new author-owned licence.
The original private Git history is intentionally not part of this public
repository. Historical commit IDs remain provenance references to that history.
