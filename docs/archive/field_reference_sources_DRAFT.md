## Confidence note

I would stake my reputation on the seven ICAR-CRIDA district Agricultural
Contingency Plans (Cuttack, Ganjam, Kendrapara for Odisha; Murshidabad, North
24-Parganas for West Bengal; East Godavari, Krishna for coastal AP) and on
ICAR-NRRI/CRRI Technology Bulletin 62 — all opened, all reproduce a printed
"Sowing window" table for rice with the exact windows quoted below. Confidence
on the ANGRAU RARS Tirupati "Package of Practices for Kharif 2014 & Rabi
2014-15" is high on the sowing/transplanting recommendation but its coverage is
the Southern Zone of AP, i.e. Chittoor/Nellore side rather than the northern
coastal (Godavari-Krishna-Guntur) belt, so it is the weakest match to "coastal
AP" as a field reference. The PMFBY National Crop Calendar has been opened and
does contain district-level Kharif rice sowing/harvest ranges, but the LLM
extraction I ran only surfaced one Bihar row; the file needs a spot-check
against the OD/WB/AP district rows before using it as a citable field reference.

**Important caveat.** These are Agricultural Contingency Plans (ICAR-CRIDA in
collaboration with OUAT, BCKV, and ANGRAU), not standalone "package of
practices" or "recommended crop calendar" documents from the state agriculture
extension directorates. They print state-university-recommended sowing windows
for rice in a "1.12 Sowing window for 5 major crops" table. If the paper needs
a document titled "Package of Practices" or "Crop Calendar" issued directly by
the Odisha DAFE, WB DoA, or AP DES rather than by ICAR-CRIDA with the SAU,
these do not fully satisfy that. Directly searching agri.odisha.gov.in,
agriculture.wb.gov.in, and des.ap.gov.in did not surface a district-resolved
kharif rice sowing/harvest calendar PDF within this pass — see "Not found"
below.

### Source 1: State: ODISHA Agriculture Contingency Plan for District: CUTTACK
- URL: https://www.icar-crida.res.in/CP/Orissa/OUAT,%20Bhubaneswar/Orissa%202-Cuttack%2031.05.2011.pdf
- Publisher / issuing body: ICAR-CRIDA in collaboration with OUAT, Bhubaneswar
  (URL path segment reads `.../OUAT, Bhubaneswar/...`). The PDF's page 1 lists
  sources including "SREP, ATMA Cuttack 2008-09" and "Orissa Agril. Statistic
  2008-09."
- Publication date: filename stamp `31.05.2011`; no publication date is
  explicitly printed on the extracted first page. Retrieved 2026-09-04.
- Geographic coverage: Cuttack district (coastal Odisha, East and South-Eastern
  Coastal Plain agro-climatic zone).
- What it gives: Section 1.12 "Sowing window for 5 major crops" gives rainfed
  and irrigated sowing windows for Paddy, Groundnut, Blackgram, Greengram,
  Sugarcane. No calendar-date harvest window; harvest recommendation is stated
  in physiological terms ("at physiological maturity stage").
- Direct quote:
  > "Kharif- Rainfed  May – June"
  > "Kharif- Irrigated  June – July"
- File format: PDF
- Downloadable: yes; saved to
  `docs/field_reference_pdfs/CRIDA_Odisha_Cuttack_31.05.2011.pdf` (483 KB)

### Source 4: CRRI Technology Bulletin 62 — Monthly Crop Calendar for Rice Cultivation
- URL: https://icar-nrri.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/75.-Crop-CalenderMonthly-Crop-Calender-for-Rice-Cultivation-_English.pdf
- Publisher / issuing body: "Published by The Director, for Central Rice
  Research Institute, Cuttack (Orissa) 753 006." (CRRI was renamed ICAR-NRRI
  in 2015; hosted on the NRRI domain.)
- Publication date: printed "September, 2009" on the document. Retrieved
  2026-09-04.
- Geographic coverage: CRRI Cuttack — coastal Odisha as the primary field
  context; applicable across rainfed upland, transplanted wet-season, and
  rainfed lowland ecologies as classified in the bulletin.
- What it gives: month-by-month calendar of operations for transplanted
  wet-season rice, rainfed upland direct-sown rice, and rainfed lowland
  direct-sown rice. Includes nursery preparation month, transplanting month,
  and harvest month.
- Direct quotes:
  > "Do nursery bed preparation during early June for transplanting in
  > favourable lowlands / irrigated medium lands / coastal saline areas"
  > "Sow seed at 20 cm apart rows behind the country plough or by seed drill
  > during middle of June." (rainfed upland)
  > "Harvest the crop when 80 per cent of the grains in panicles got matured
  > (at physiological maturity stage)" — listed under October, 95–100 DAT for
  > transplanted wet-season rice.
- File format: PDF
- Downloadable: yes on the public web (fetched successfully via the Perplexity
  content fetch gateway). The sandbox `curl` failed DNS on `icar-nrri.in`, so
  the file is NOT saved locally under `docs/field_reference_pdfs/`. The URL
  above resolves from a normal browser; retry on a network that can reach
  `icar-nrri.in`.
